If the work outlives a chat, don't keep the plan in the chat.

`fest next` hands the next task. `camp` keeps the repos from turning into a junk drawer.

Both are [Obedience Corp](https://obediencecorp.com) tools. Loop docs: [fest.build](https://fest.build).
